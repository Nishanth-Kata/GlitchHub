// 02 · workflow

How it works

From download to first upload in a few minutes. No website signup. Install the app, set up Google OAuth, then sign in.

01

Download & install

Download for your platform and run the installer. GlitchHub starts a local server on 127.0.0.1:49152. Nothing leaves your machine until you sign in.

02

Setup Google OAuth

On first launch, click Setup Google OAuth and add your Google Cloud Desktop client ID to desktop-config.json. Restart the app when done.

03

Connect Google Drive

Click Continue with Google. Your browser opens for a one-time consent. Tokens are encrypted and stored locally in SQLite.

04

Capture & organize

Create your Project / Task / Round. Drop screenshots into the media tray. Everything is auto-uploaded into the right Drive folder, ready to share.

// 05 · questions

FAQ

Where is my data stored?

Screenshots and evidence are uploaded to your Google Drive. Auth tokens are stored encrypted in a local SQLite database at %APPDATA%\GlitchHub\db\auth.db.

Do you host anything?

We host this landing page and the download binary. That's it. The app itself talks only to Google APIs and your local machine.

Can I use my own Google OAuth credentials?

Yes. Open Settings inside the app or use the enterprise setup wizard on the Welcome screen. See the Enterprise section for details.

What port does GlitchHub use?

127.0.0.1:49152. If another app is using that port, GlitchHub will refuse to start. Close the conflicting app and try again.

How do I update?

Installed GlitchHub checks for updates automatically on launch. When a new version is available, a card appears in the bottom-right corner of the app — click Update, then Restart to install. You can also open Settings → Check for updates.
