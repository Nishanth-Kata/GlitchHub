// 05 · questions

FAQ

Where is my data stored, and is it private?

All your notes, screenshots, and test evidence are stored locally on your machine and synced directly to your own Google Drive storage. We run zero database servers—no third party ever has access to your credentials or files.

Do I need a paid subscription or dedicated server?

No. GlitchHub is completely serverless. You can use your existing free Google Account (15GB included storage) or your company’s Google Workspace plan without any subscription fees or third-party cloud lock-in.

How does authentication work? Can we use custom Google OAuth?

GlitchHub uses secure OAuth 2.0 loopback authentication (127.0.0.1:49152). Tokens are stored encrypted in your local SQLite database at %APPDATA%\GlitchHub\db\auth.db. Enterprise teams can also import a custom .glitchhuborg config to route authentication through their own Google Cloud Console OAuth Client.

How does GlitchHub handle network connectivity and ports?

GlitchHub runs as a standalone desktop application. You can write documentation, capture media, and organize tasks continuously. Background synchronization keeps your data safe while the local client operates on loopback port 127.0.0.1:49152.

How do app updates work?

GlitchHub automatically checks for new releases on startup. When an update is detected, an in-app notification banner allows you to click Update & Restart. You can also manually trigger an update check anytime via Settings → Check for updates.
